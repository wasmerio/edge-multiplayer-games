TRAPDOOR

connecting all games

A floor show for 2 to 8 players

A shape gets called. Stand on it. Everything else is a trapdoor.

  1. Find it. The floor reshuffles on every call and the clock gets shorter.
  2. Keep it. Dash into a rival to shove them off their plate, or into the pit.
  3. Fell in? Haunt the floor and crack a plate under the survivors.
