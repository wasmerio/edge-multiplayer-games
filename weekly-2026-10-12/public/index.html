The memory workshop / 2–8 weavers

A fleeting pattern.
A race to remember.

Watch a stitch pattern disappear, then weave it back from memory before your friends.

A Loop · S Peak · D Cross
Tap one key at a time. On a phone, use the three stitch buttons.

  1. Watch. Memorise 3–6 stitches, one at a time.
  2. Weave. Repeat the order. A wrong stitch unravels your work; you can retry.
  3. Outweave. Finish for 3 points, first for 5. The last two patterns pay double.

Four patterns per round. First to 50 with a clear lead wins. No one sits out.
