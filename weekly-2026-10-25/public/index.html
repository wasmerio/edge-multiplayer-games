Rotate the tracks to connect power to the lamp. Repair four circuits faster than your friends.

▶✦ IN → CONNECT → LIGHT
  1. Select. Left / Right moves through the nine tiles, wrapping at the ends.
  2. Rotate. Tap Space to turn the selected track clockwise. Mint tracks carry power.
  3. Repair. Join the triangle at top left to the square lamp at bottom right. Spare tracks can stay loose.

45 seconds · 5 points per repair · +2 for first · last two circuits pay 10 · first to 60 with a clear lead wins.
