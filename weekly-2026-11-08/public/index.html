Rotate nine copper circuits to light every lamp before your friends.

Arrows / WASD select. Space turns.
On a phone, use the stick and TURN pad.

  1. Follow the power. It enters the top-left tile. Touching pipe ends light the next lamp.
  2. Finish the circuit. All nine lit earns 5 points. First finish earns 2 extra. You have 30 seconds.
  3. Keep the lights on. Unfinished? Three lit earns 1; six earns 2. First to 20 with a clear lead wins.
